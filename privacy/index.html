Allerio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Allerio is an iPhone app that checks a product barcode against ingredient filters you choose. There is no account.

Data stored on your iPhone

Ingredient filters, your scan plan, and purchase history are saved on the device. Allerio does not receive your Apple ID or payment card details. Apple processes purchases. Deleting the app deletes this on-device data.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Photos are not uploaded.

Open Food Facts

The barcode is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product name and ingredients. That request includes the barcode and your IP address, which is normal for a web request. Allerio does not sell this data.

Crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, Firebase Crashlytics may receive a crash report: the app version, device model, operating system version, and a diagnostic stack trace. Reports are used to fix crashes. They are not used for advertising and are not linked to your name.

Children

Allerio is not directed at children under 13.
